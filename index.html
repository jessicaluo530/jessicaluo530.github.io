B.S. in Statistics · University of Michigan, Ann Arbor

Hi, I'm Jessica! I am an undergraduate student in Statistics at the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor. I work with Professors Jun Li and Stefanus Jasin in Technology and Operations at the Ross School of Business, and with collaborators at Arizona State University, Ohio State, and HKUST.

I am interested in how AI, incentives, and information shape decisions in markets and digital platforms. I like to start close to a market, learning what its participants want, what constrains them, and what each knows that the others do not, and then abstract the mechanism into a model or an empirical framework. In practice, that means building models and testing them with data, or starting from an empirical pattern and building the model that explains it.

I am currently applying to PhD programs in operations management.
